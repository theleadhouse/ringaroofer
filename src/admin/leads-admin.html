How to use the Lead Desk

Overview: today's picture. Leads, how many buyers wanted, how many sold, money in, and margin after costs.

Leads: every form submission. Click one to see each buyer's answer in order: Match means the buyer wanted it and bid a price, Accepted means it was sold, red means rejected with the reason. Use Download log to send a buyer the exact ping and post.

Buyers: each buyer's bids, acceptance rate and money. TEST means connected to their test system; LIVE means real sales. "Send test lead" only works while a buyer is in test.

Sources & margin: which site, ad channel or publisher makes money.

Money: enter ad spend; see what each buyer owes us and what we owe publishers.

Every lead is offered to all connected buyers at the same moment. The highest bid wins; if that buyer rejects the full lead, the next best buyer gets it automatically.
