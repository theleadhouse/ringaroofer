FAQ

Questions roofing companies ask

What is RingaRoofer?

A referral service. Homeowners call us, and we connect each call to an independent roofing company that serves their area.

What kind of calls would I get?

Homeowners calling about the roofing work you tell us you take on, in the areas you serve. We don't promise a set number of calls; volume depends on your area and the season.

Do homeowners pay RingaRoofer?

No. Roofing companies in our network pay for the connections, on terms agreed with each company.

Who agrees the work with the homeowner?

You do, directly. We don't set your terms or do the work.

What do you look for in a roofing company?

A real local business that answers its phone, serves the areas it lists, does the work it says it does, and holds the credentials and coverage its state and city require.

What happens after I send my details?

We review them and reach out by phone or email to talk through areas, services and terms. There's no obligation.
