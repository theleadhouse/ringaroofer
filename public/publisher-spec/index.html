How it works

0. Pre-ping (free)
Send only category + ZIP. We answer yes or no instantly, without contacting any buyer. Skip pinging anything we answer "no" to.
1. Ping
Send the lead without name, phone or email. We check our buyers in real time and answer with our bid in USD.
2. Post
If you accept the bid, send the full lead with the ping_id within 2 minutes.
3. Result
We answer accepted (you are paid the bid) or rejected with the reason. You are paid only for accepted leads.

Your account starts in TEST: everything works the same, but leads only reach our test buyer and nothing is billed. We switch you to LIVE after a successful test. Use the test console to try it without code.

Limits

Every key has a speed limit (default 60 requests per minute, pre-pings and pings together) and can have hourly and daily caps on sold leads. When you hit one we answer at once:

HTTP 429  { "status": "rejected", "reason": "rate limit: max 60 requests per minute", "retry_after": 60 }
HTTP 200  { "status": "rejected", "reason": "daily cap reached", "retry_after": 41230 }

GET /v1/coverage shows your categories, what is open now and how much of your caps is left. Quality is checked automatically: if buyers reject too many of your recent leads, your key is paused and we contact you.

Rules

  • Only real consumers who requested contact on your page. No incentivized, co-registration or purchased lists.
  • Every lead needs a valid TrustedForm certificate from the same session and the approved consent wording.
  • Duplicate phone numbers within 30 days are rejected. Leads can be returned by buyers for invalid contact details; returned leads are deducted.
  • Payment: monthly, for accepted leads net of returns, per your agreement.
  • Consumers who opt out (any of our pages, /v1/optout, or told to you) must be honored within 10 business days. Send us opt-outs you receive: POST /v1/optout {"phone": "..."}.
  • We keep consent records (certificate, consent text, page, IP, time) for 5 years. Keep yours for at least as long.
  • Questions: email us and include your ping_id.
