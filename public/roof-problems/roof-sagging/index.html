What you can do

  • Keep people out of rooms below.

  • Note any sounds like creaking.

  • Don't shovel a sagging roof yourself.

What a roofer does, and what to tell them

What a roofer usually does

  • Checks decking and framing from the attic.
  • Replaces wet or weakened decking.
  • Brings in a structural engineer if framing needs it.

Questions people ask

Roof looks like it's sagging: 12 questions people ask

Is a sagging roof dangerous?

It can be. Treat it as urgent.

What causes sagging?

Water-soaked decking, weak framing or heavy snow.

Can it be fixed without a new roof?

Sometimes; a roofer will assess the structure.

Can heavy snow make a roof sag?

Yes, especially on older framing or flat roofs.

How many layers of shingles are too many?

Local codes limit it, often to two. Extra layers add weight.

Can rotten decking cause sagging?

Yes. Wet decking weakens and dips between rafters.

Who fixes the framing?

Roofers handle decking; framing may need a carpenter or a structural engineer's plan.

Can I see sagging from the ground?

Look along the ridge and roof lines for dips or waves.

Does an old roof always sag?

No. Sagging points to a specific cause worth finding.

Can a sagging roof be fixed in winter?

Temporary support can be added; permanent repairs depend on weather.

Is a slight wave in shingles a sag?

Not always. It can be uneven decking. A roofer can tell the difference.

Should I stay out of the attic?

If the roof is sagging, yes, until it's checked.
