The short answer

  • Look for dented vents, metal trim and outdoor equipment.
  • Granules piling up at downspouts can be a sign.
  • A roofer can check up close for bruised shingles.

From the ground

What to check first

Stay off the roof. Everything here can be done from the ground, inside, or from a safe spot in the attic.

  1. 1

    Look for dents on vents, metal trim and AC units.

  2. 2

    Check downspout outlets for shingle grit.

  3. 3

    Look for dark spots where granules are missing.

  4. 4

    Photograph hail size with a coin for scale.

  5. 5

    Note the storm date.

What to do now

What you can do

  • Photograph hail and dents.
  • Note the date.
  • Get the roof checked.
  • Get written findings.
  • Keep records.

What to avoid

  • Don't climb up yourself.
  • Don't sign anything on the spot.
  • Don't let anyone rush you.

What a roofer usually does

  • Checks shingles, metal, vents and flashing up close.
  • Marks and photographs hail hits.
  • Recommends repair or replacement based on what they find.

Questions people ask

Hail hit your roof: 11 questions people ask

Can hail hurt a roof without leaks?

Yes. Bruised shingles can take weeks or months to start leaking.

What size hail is a concern?

Larger hail is more likely to bruise shingles; a roofer can tell up close.

Should I trust door-to-door roofers after hail?

Be careful. The FTC advises checking out contractors before you hire.

Can hail problems show up later?

Bruised shingles can lose granules and wear faster over time.

Should I photograph hail?

Yes. Hailstones next to a coin or ruler, and the roof from the ground, help.

What signs of hail can I see from the ground?

Dents on vents, metal edges and outdoor equipment, and piles of granules at downspouts.

Does hail affect metal roofs?

It can dent them. Some dents are only cosmetic; a roofer can tell you.

Can I go on the roof to look?

No. Let a roofer check it safely.

Does hail affect tile roofs?

It can crack tiles, especially older ones.

How soon should a roofer check after hail?

Within a few days is wise, and sooner if anything leaks.

What should I write down after a hailstorm?

Date, time, hail size, what you saw, and photos. Our after-storm notes sheet helps.

Words you may hear

Hail bruise
A soft spot on a shingle where hail cracked the mat underneath, sometimes with granules knocked off.
Drip edge
Metal edging along the roof's edges that directs water away from the edge boards.
Ridge
The top line where two roof slopes meet.
Rake
The sloped edge of a roof at the end of a gable.
Gable
The triangular wall at the end of a pitched roof.
