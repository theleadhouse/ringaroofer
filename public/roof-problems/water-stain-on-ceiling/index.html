Water stain on the ceiling?

A brown ring on the ceiling can be a roof leak, a plumbing leak or condensation. Here's how to tell which.

The short answer

  • Stains on top-floor ceilings after rain point to the roof.
  • Stains under a bathroom point to plumbing.
  • Mark the edge and watch whether it grows.

From the ground

What to check first

Stay off the roof. Everything here can be done from the ground, inside, or from a safe spot in the attic.

  1. 1

    Is the room on the top floor?

  2. 2

    Does it appear or grow after rain?

  3. 3

    Is there a bathroom or pipes above it?

  4. 4

    Is it near an exterior wall or vent?

  5. 5

    Trace the stain edge in pencil and check after the next rain.

What to do now

What you can do

  • Mark the stain edge.
  • Check after rain.
  • Look in the attic if it's safe.
  • Photograph it.
  • Book the right trade.

What to avoid

  • Don't just paint over it.
  • Don't ignore a growing stain.
  • Don't poke a soft ceiling.

When you call

What a roofer does, and what to tell them

What a roofer usually does

  • Checks the attic above the stain for wet wood or insulation.
  • Rules out plumbing or air conditioning lines.
  • Traces the leak uphill on the roof.
  • Repairs it and tells you when the ceiling can be painted.

What to tell the roofer

“There's a water stain on my ceiling in room. It grows after rain / is the same size. There's a bathroom / nothing above it.”

Fill in the highlighted parts. A clear description helps the roofer tell you what it likely involves.

Questions people ask

Water stain on the ceiling: 11 questions people ask

Is a ceiling stain always a roof leak?

No. Plumbing and condensation can also cause stains.

How do I know if the leak is active?

Mark the edge; if it grows after rain, it's active.

Can I paint over a water stain?

Fix the source first, then use a stain-blocking primer.

Does a stain mean mold?

Not always, but areas that stay wet can grow mold. Fix the leak and dry it.

How do I know the leak is fixed?

Mark the stain's edge in pencil and watch it through the next few rains.

Why is the stain brown?

Water picks up color from wood, insulation and dust as it travels.

Can a stain come from attic condensation?

Yes. In winter, moisture in the attic can drip and stain ceilings below.

Is a stain near an outside wall a roof leak?

It can be the roof edge, an ice dam, or wall flashing. A roofer can trace it.

Will the drywall need replacing?

Small, dried stains can be primed. Soft or sagging drywall usually needs replacing.

Should I check the attic myself?

If it's safe to get to, look for wet wood or insulation above the stain. Step only on framing.

Why did the stain appear in dry weather?

It may be a plumbing or air conditioning leak, or condensation in the attic.

Words you may hear

Underlayment
A water-resistant layer under the shingles or tiles that's a second line of defense against leaks.
Counter flashing
Flashing set into or over a wall that covers the top of the step flashing.
Dormer
A structure that sticks out of a sloped roof, often with its own small roof.
Roof penetration
Anything that goes through the roof: vents, pipes, fans. Each one needs a good seal.
