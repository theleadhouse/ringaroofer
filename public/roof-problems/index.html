Winter & attic

Book a visit this week

Ice dams or leaks in winter?

Thick ice at the roof edge can trap melting snow, which backs up under shingles and leaks into ceilings.

See what to check Book a visit this week

Damp attic or musty smell?

Wet insulation, frost on nails or a musty smell in the attic can be a roof leak, poor ventilation or warm air leaking in.

See what to check Get help today

Roof looks like it's sagging?

A dip or wave in the roof line can mean weak decking or framing, sometimes made worse by heavy snow.
