Wear & aging

Shingle grit at the downspouts?

Granules wash off asphalt shingles as they age, or after hail. A little is normal; a lot can mean the roof is wearing out.

The short answer

  • Some grit is normal, especially on a new roof.
  • Heavy grit plus bald spots means wear.
  • After hail, it can mean bruised shingles.

From the ground

What to check first

Stay off the roof. Everything here can be done from the ground, inside, or from a safe spot in the attic.

  1. 1

    Look at downspout outlets after rain.

  2. 2

    Look for dark, bald patches on shingles.

  3. 3

    Note the roof's age.

  4. 4

    Note recent hail.

  5. 5

    Photograph the grit.

Practical steps

What to do now

What you can do

  • Note the roof's age.
  • Photograph the grit.
  • Book an inspection if it's heavy.
  • Plan ahead for replacement.
  • Keep records.

What to avoid

  • Don't climb up to look.
  • Don't ignore bald patches.
  • Don't panic over a little grit on a new roof.

  • Checks how much granule wear the shingles have.
  • Looks for bald spots and hail bruising.

Questions people ask

Shingle grit at the downspouts: 12 questions people ask

Is shingle grit normal?

A little is normal, especially on a new roof.

What do bald spots mean?

Granules protect shingles from sun; bald spots wear faster.

Does grit mean I need a new roof?

Not always; a roofer can tell you how much life is left.

What are roof granules?

The colored grit on asphalt shingles that protects them from the sun.

Is grit after a new roof normal?

A little, yes. It's loose extra grit from manufacturing.

Can hail knock granules off?

Yes. A sudden pile of grit after hail is a reason to have the roof checked.

Can I see bald spots from the ground?

Sometimes, as darker or shinier patches. Binoculars help.

Does pressure washing remove granules?

Yes. Avoid pressure washing asphalt shingles.

Why does one side lose more grit?

The sunniest slope usually ages fastest.

Should I check downspouts after storms?

Yes. Lots of fresh grit after hail or high wind is worth having checked.

Is grit from a roof harmful?

It's mostly mineral grit; just clean it out of drains.

Can a roofer tell how much life is left?

They can look at granule loss and other signs to give you a rough idea.

Words you may hear

Granules
The colored grit on asphalt shingles that protects them from sunlight. Losing lots of them is a sign of wear.
Architectural shingles
Thicker, layered asphalt shingles with a dimensional look. Also called laminated or dimensional shingles.
Three-tab shingles
Flat asphalt shingles with three tabs per strip; thinner than architectural shingles.
Blistering
Small bubbles on asphalt shingles that can pop and lose granules.
Course
A single row of shingles across the roof.
Exposure
The part of each shingle you can see once the next row is on.
